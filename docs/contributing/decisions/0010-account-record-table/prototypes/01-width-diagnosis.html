Account console · density study

The detail pane gets 424 px of a 1152 px page.

Widening your browser does not help. The account shell is capped at 72rem, so past 1152 px every extra pixel becomes margin. That cap is the whole bug — the wrapped UUIDs are just what it looks like.

Where the 1152 px goes

40 nav 200 62 list 352 24 detail 434 40
  • 62% navigation, filters, gutters
  • 38% the record you came to read
  • 115px per metadata column at columns={3}
  • 36 chars in a UUID

Three separate decisions stack into that 434 px, and each one is independently fixable:

The cap. AccountManagementShell uses layoutMaxWidths.extended — 72rem. That is a reading measure, right for prose and wrong for a three-column console. The rail costs another 262 px on every account page.

The column count. <MetadataGrid columns={3}> is a fixed number, so it divides whatever it is given. Three columns of 115 px is strictly worse than one column of 376 px, and the component has no way to know.

The ids. A UUID reaches the grid with overflow-wrap: anywhere, which treats it as prose. It is not prose — nobody reads a UUID, they copy it. That is a different component.
